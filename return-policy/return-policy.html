Returns & Exchanges Policy

This policy applies to orders that have already shipped. All items must be unused and returned in their original condition to be eligible for a return or exchange.

Customers are responsible for verifying product specifications, selections, and compatibility before placing an order. Incorrectly ordered products are subject to the applicable return or exchange terms and fees listed below.

Original shipping charges are non-refundable. Additional shipping costs associated with a return or exchange are the responsibility of the customer.

Chassis

  • Returns for Refund: A $35 restocking fee applies.
  • Exchanges: A $15 shipping fee applies.

Barrels

  • Returns for Refund: A $35 restocking fee applies.
  • Exchanges: A $35 restocking fee applies. If the barrel requires remanufacturing to complete the exchange, an additional $65 remanufacturing fee will apply.

Complete the Returns & Exchanges Form

Our team will follow up with you within 3 business days.

Order Changes

Since our barrels are made to order, changes depend on the current stage of production.

  • Production not started: No fee to modify.
  • Production started: Modifying the order means starting a new barrel from scratch, so a $65 remanufacturing fee + $35 restocking fee applies. Canceling requires a $35 restocking fee.

Order Cancellations

Since our barrels are made to order, cancellation depends on the current stage of production.

  • Production not started: No fee to cancel.
  • Production started: Canceling requires a $35 restocking fee.

Missing Item

First, check your packing slip. Your order may have been split into multiple shipments so available items could ship sooner. The packing slip will only list the items included in your current shipment; any remaining items will ship separately at a later date.

If your packing slip shows that an item should have been included in the shipment but is missing, please complete the Missing Item Report below. Our team will review your request and follow up with further steps within 3 business days.
