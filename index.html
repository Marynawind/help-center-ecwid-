Barrels

What barrel lengths do you offer?

Match Precision barrels: 16 ", 18", 20", 22", 24", and 26"

Backdraft barrels: 26"

Carbon Fiber - Mirage Reducing Barrel: 20”, 26”

What materials are your barrels made from?

Premium 416R stainless steel.

Can I request an action or chamber that isn’t listed on your website?

Our barrels are chambered and threaded only in the configurations listed on our website. Due to our current machining and programming processes, we’re unable to accommodate custom chambering, threading, or other custom product requests.

If you need a configuration we don’t currently offer, you can purchase a complete barrel blank and have it chambered and threaded by a qualified gunsmith. Barrels must either be ordered as a complete blank or fully chambered and threaded by Outlier USA.

What are the specs on bolt action barrels?

All bolt action barrels are 1.2" outer diameter (OD), either straight bull or MTU contour, unshouldered, with 5/8-24 TPI muzzle threading.

What are the specs on Backdraft barrels?

All Backdraft barrels have a 1.20" OD across all calibers, with the exception of AR9 and AR15 barrels, which have a 1.0" OD.

What is the difference between the Bull and MTU contour?

The Bull contour is a straight contour, holding the same 1.2" OD along its full length. The MTU contour has a slight taper toward the end of the barrel.

What is the MTU contour?

MTU stands for Marksmanship Training Unit — the contour was designed to the specs that the Marksmanship Unit requested for their rifles. Chamber diameter is 1.20", and muzzle diameter grows as the barrel gets shorter:

What barrel nut do your barrels use, and how do I install it?

All bolt action barrels come with a proprietary barrel nut fitted to the selected pre-fit. The nut is 1.30" across the flats — you can use any 1.30" open-end wrench or straight claw crowfoot to install the barrel. To set headspace, make sure you have a known-good set of headspace gauges.

Which barrel nut wrench should I use with my Outlier bolt action barrel?

A 33mm (1.30") crowfoot wrench — here's a link to one on Amazon.

How do I set the headspace?

Our barrels use the provided barrel nut to set headspace. You'll need a known-good set of headspace gauges, a 33mm open-end wrench, and a barrel vice. If you are not comfortable with the installation or headspacing process, we recommend having a qualified gunsmith perform the installation.

What torque should I use on the barrel nut?

For our barrel nut system, we recommend 40–55 ft-lbs of torque for most receivers. We do not recommend using the receiver manufacturer’s barrel torque specification, as those specifications are typically intended for a shouldered barrel installation rather than a barrel nut system.

Should I get a Savage Large or Savage Small pre-fit?

Most Savage rifles use the Savage Small Shank. To confirm which pre-fit you need, measure your existing barrel nut to determine whether your rifle uses a Small or Large Shank.

  • Small Shank: barrel is 1.055", nut is 7/8" wide
  • Large Shank: barrel is 1.120", nut is 5/8" wide
Which actions are you not compatible with?

Common actions we do not accommodate are Browning X-Bolt, Coup de Grace, Defiance, Curtis, and Faxon FX7.

Do I need to break in my new bolt action barrel?

No special break-in process is required — clean it to your liking and go shoot.

What's the maintenance protocol for Backdraft barrels?

For optimal performance and reduced carbon buildup, apply one squirt of Backdraft Carbon Defense into each of the 3 barrel channels before shooting, and again after shooting. A sample bottle will be included with your barrel order.

Backdraft Carbon Defense is a water-based carbon solvent designed to help reduce carbon fouling and unburnt particulate accumulation within the barrel channel system. Regular use helps protect against buildup to ensure clean and safe operation.

After shooting, allow the barrel to cool before applying cleaner. Excess residue may be removed with compressed air, patches, or a cleaning brush as needed.

Chassis

How do I get my free chassis with a barrel purchase?

Add the chassis of your choice to your cart along with your barrel. The chassis discount will be automatically applied at checkout, making the chassis free.

What chassis fits my action?

Chassis compatibility depends on your specific action and configuration. Review our most common compatibility questions below.

Which actions are compatible with the Remington 700 pre-fit chassis?

The Remington 700 pre-fit shares the same footprint across: Rem 700 SA, Aero Solus, Bergara, Christensen Arms, Curtis, Faxon FX7, Impact, Terminus Zeus, and Weatherby 307.

Is the Ruger chassis compatible with the Ruger American Gen1 and Gen2?

Yes. The Gen1 and Gen2 does not change the action/chassis footprint interface.

Which model do I get for my Savage action?

Savage SA and Savage LA have different footprints, so you'll need to pick the right one for your action:

  • Savage SA: Model 110-10, 11, 12, 14, 16 — 3.0" magwell, for small cartridges
  • Savage LA: Model 110, 111, 112, 114, 116 — 3.715"/3.850" magwell, for large cartridges

Savage Axis - both short and long actions share the same footprint. The only thing that changes is magwell length: short action 3.0" vs. long action 3.56".

Will your chassis work with the Savage bolt release?

Our chassis is designed to work with both top and bottom bolt release designs. The bottom release can be engaged through the side slot in the chassis (tool not included).

Which Tikka models will the Tikka chassis fit?

T, T1, T3, and T3x — both short action 3.0”, and long action 3.56”, differing by magwell length.

How to Redeem the Free Suppressor Rebate?
  1. Once you have a redemption code, visit BackdraftSuppressors.com
  2. Select a 4” Hunter, or upgrade to 6”, 8”, or steel Poacher for an additional cost.
  3. Select the required HUB Adapter*.
  4. Enter the rebate code at checkout.
  5. Select your preferred FFL/SOT Dealer for suppressor transfer.
  6. Complete checkout and pay all required charges, including any upgrade cost, HUB Adapter, shipping and taxes.
