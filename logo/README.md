## How it was made

The only original is a 750×350 raster, so it was redrawn as vectors:

- **The Outlier oval and wordmark** are the existing vector traced from the
  2474px master on dealers.getoutlier.com (the Help Center watermark). It fits
  the original at one uniform scale — oval and wordmark land within a pixel —
  so its shape is exact.
- **BACKDRAFT SUPPRESSORS BY** is traced from `original-750x350.png`:
  upscaled 8×, cleaned of single-pixel spikes, turned into curves.

Side by side at the original size the two are indistinguishable. At 10×
magnification the lettering shows very slight waviness from the ~55px-tall
source letters — invisible on a business card.
